MINEVIL

The cheapest anvil order to put every enchantment you want on one item in Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

BEDROCK EDITION RULES

1. Pick your item

2. Pick enchantments

Choose an item first.

Click a name to add it at max level, or click a numeral to set the level. Conflicting enchantments are crossed out.

3. Cheapest anvil order

Pick an item and at least one enchantment.

Rules used

  • Each anvil step costs: enchantment levels × book multiplier + prior-work penalty of both sides (2uses − 1).
  • Any step costing 40 or more levels is "Too Expensive" in survival, so the calculator rejects those orders.
  • Bedrock has no Sweeping Edge. Infinity and Mending can share a bow. Incompatible enchantments are dropped for free, so conflicts are blocked up front.
  • The search tests every merge order, then picks the lowest total levels. XP points assume you pay each step from level 0; real points depend on your starting level.
  • Spear and Lunge enchantment pools are the newest data here; double-check them in your world.
